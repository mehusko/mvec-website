Who we are

MVEC is a Malé based employment consultancy. We connect families and businesses in the Maldives with skilled workers from abroad, and we handle the paperwork on Xpat from quota to arrival.

For households, that means housemaids, nannies, cooks and caregivers you can trust. For businesses, it means expat quota, work permits, visas and renewals handled on time.
