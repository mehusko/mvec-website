Safety and vetting

Bringing someone into your home is a big decision. Here is exactly how we screen every worker before presenting them to your family, so you know who you're hiring.

How we screen every worker

These are the checks a candidate goes through before we present them to you.

  1. 1

    Identity and passport

    We check the passport and identity documents of every candidate, and that they match the CV.

  2. 2

    Police clearance

    Each candidate provides a police clearance certificate from their home country.

  3. 3

    Medical fitness

    A medical check in the home country before travel, and the required medical again on arrival in the Maldives.

  4. 4

    References and work history

    We contact previous employers where possible and confirm the experience on the CV.

  5. 5

    Interview

    We interview every candidate, and you can do a video interview yourself before you decide.

  6. 6

    After arrival

    Work permit, insurance and visa are done properly, and we stay in touch with you and the worker after they start.
